## LingLib coverage count

“550+ works in semantics covered” is a conservative rounded count of 551 entries in the live LingLib bibliography tagged `role: formalized` and `area: semantics`, checked on 25 October 2026 at https://linglib.io/bibliography/. The bibliography includes books and theses as well as papers; its cited-only and foundational entries are excluded. This count refers to works represented in the library, not a claim that every result in each work has been formalized.
